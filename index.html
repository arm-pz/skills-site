input — launch post excerpt

"Nova is the world's most advanced writing tool. Thousands of companies already use it every day to transform their business. In today's fast-paced digital landscape, it is more important than ever to stay ahead."

findings

  • high"world's most advanced", "thousands of companies" — factual claims with no source. Cite or cut; both are load-bearing.
  • med"transform their business" — vague benefit. Say what actually changes, for whom.
  • med"In today's fast-paced digital landscape" — stock opener carrying zero information. Delete the clause.

house rules

What the editor never does.

Preserve meaning

Names, numbers, dates, quotations, hedges and the author's voice survive every edit unless you ask otherwise.

Least intervention

A proof when a proof is enough. Rewrites are deliberate, explained, and never silent.

No invention

No fabricated sources, no claims dressed as verified, no new facts slipped into your prose.
