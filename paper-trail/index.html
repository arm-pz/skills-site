Paper Trail

Privacy policy, terms, refund and cookie pages written only from facts you confirm, then checked for placeholders, missing sections and emails nobody supplied. Says when a lawyer should look.

6 files and 382 lines, free under MIT. Built from arm-pz/caffeinity-skills at b169944.

What it is for

Drafts the policy pages a small product needs before it takes money or data (privacy policy, terms of service, refund policy, cookie notice) from a fact sheet about what the product actually collects, stores, shares and charges, then checks the drafts for placeholders, missing sections and facts nobody supplied. Not legal advice and not for contracts, NDAs or employment documents (use a lawyer), and not for the launch plan itself (use solo-income-blueprint) or the site build (use site-prompt).

Reach for it when

  • Someone needs a privacy policy, terms, refund or cookie page for a site, app, SaaS, newsletter or digital product, asks what legal pages they need before launch, or wants existing policy pages checked against what the product really does, even if they never say "legal".
