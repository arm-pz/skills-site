## Why we collect it

- **Account data:** to create and run your account, log you in, and email you about the service.
- **Access tokens, payout and invoice records:** to match your payouts to your invoices, which is the service you asked for. The tokens are read-only.
- **Analytics:** to see which pages are used.
- **Error reports:** to find and fix faults.
- **Payment details:** to take your subscription payment through Stripe.
