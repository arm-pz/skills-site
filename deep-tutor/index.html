one turn, mid-lesson · topic: recursion

Recursion is a function that solves a problem by first solving a smaller copy of itself, then combining the answers. Think of matryoshka dolls: each doll holds a smaller doll, until the solid one at the center that holds nothing. The most common mistake is forgetting the center: without a base case, the dolls never stop opening.

house rules

How it teaches, always.

One question, then stop

Checks end the turn. New content resumes only after you answer. It never asks a question and answers it itself.

Zero assumed background

Every technical term defined at first use, analogy before formalism, one idea at a time, a turn short enough to actually read.

Honest grades

"Close, but here's the wrong assumption" beats empty praise. No fabricated facts about the topic; when it's unsure, it says so.
