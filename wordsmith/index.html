house rules

What the editor never does.

Preserve meaning

Names, numbers, dates, quotations, hedges and the author's voice survive every edit unless you ask otherwise.

Least intervention

A proof when a proof is enough. Rewrites are deliberate, explained, and never silent.

No invention

No fabricated sources, no claims dressed as verified, no new facts slipped into your prose.

Give your agent taste for prose.

Free, open, and it runs entirely inside the agent you already use.
