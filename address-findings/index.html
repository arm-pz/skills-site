What it is for

Fixes the CRITICAL and HIGH findings in a consistent-code-review report for one pull request, staying inside the files that PR already changes, then reports each finding as fixed, won't fix (with evidence) or escalate.

Reach for it when

  • A review-report.json exists for a PR and its blocking findings need addressing, or when the pr-graph conductor calls the fixer node.

Not for

  • Writing a review (use consistent-code-review)
  • Adding features, rebasing for conflicts, or merging (merge_gate.py decides that)
