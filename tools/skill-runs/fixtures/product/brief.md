# Product brief: Reconcile (by Ledgerline)
What it is: a tool that matches Stripe and GoCardless payouts to invoices in Xero automatically.
Who buys it: finance leads at UK SaaS companies with 200–5,000 invoices a month, usually a 1–3 person finance team.
Pain: month-end reconciliation takes 2–4 days by hand; mismatched payouts (fees, refunds, partial payments) are found weeks late.
Proof we can use: 31 paying customers; median customer cut month-end close from 3.5 days to 1 day (our own onboarding survey, n=24). Quote allowed: "We closed October in a day for the first time." — Hana Ito, Finance Lead, Stackwise.
Price: £149/month up to 1,000 invoices, £349/month up to 5,000. 14-day free trial, no card.
Not true / do not claim: no QuickBooks integration yet; not SOC 2 certified (audit in progress); no AI.
Goal: free-trial sign-ups from a landing page and a 3-email sequence for people who downloaded our month-end checklist.
Voice: plain, specific, a little dry. No hype.
