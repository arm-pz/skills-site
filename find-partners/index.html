What it is for

Finds real, non-competing businesses and publishers that share your audience, such as complementary tools, agencies, newsletters and podcasts, screens out link sellers, picks one collaboration per partner (guest post, expert quote, co-made data, integration listing, podcast) and drafts the ask, written to a partners CSV and one ask file per partner. Use whenever someone wants partners for backlinks or exposure: find sites to collaborate with, guest post opportunities, who could we co-market with, swap links for me, find link exchange partners, even if they never say partnership. Link-swap requests get the collaboration that earns the link without a swap. Step 3, the last of a three-step handover after replace-dead-tools.

Not for

  • Best-of list outreach (use get-listed)
  • Dead entries on lists (use replace-dead-tools)
  • Competitor research (use research-brief)
