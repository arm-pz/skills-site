How it works

From call to working skill.

  1. Call

    Thirty minutes, free. You show us the work you want an agent to do; we tell you whether a skill will help.

  2. Scope

    A one-page plan: which skills, what they do, what they never do, and how we'll test them. You approve before we start.

  3. Build and test

    We write the skills and run them on your real tasks, the same way every skill on this site is tested.

  4. Hand over

    You get the repo, install steps and a walkthrough. The skills are yours to keep and change.

Questions

Before you write.

Who owns the skills you build?

You do. They're delivered in a private repo under the license you choose. We don't publish them here unless you ask us to.

Do you need access to our systems or data?

No. We work from example tasks you share, with anything sensitive removed. We never need production access or your API keys.

Which agents do the skills work with?

Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Qoder and any agent that reads a SKILL.md. Tell us which ones your team uses and we test on those.

Why pay when the skills on this site are free?

The free skills cover general work. A custom skill knows your workflow, your rules and your tools, and someone has tested it on your tasks. The free skills stay free.

Can we see what your work looks like first?

Yes. Every skill on this site is open on GitHub with its tests, so you can read exactly how we build before you book a call.
