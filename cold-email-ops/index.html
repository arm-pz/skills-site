Cold Email Ops

Plan and audit a cold-email program at volume and keep it from degrading: computes inbox and domain capacity from a monthly send target, sets per-domain reply-rate floors, burn-replacement cadence, shadow-ban tests, and automation error budgets, and reports prospects-to-lead with per-client averages. Use when someone is scaling outbound, asks how many inboxes or domains they need, why reply rates are falling or a domain might be shadow banned, or reviews an AI outbound stack for failure points. Not for pre-load list QA (cold-email-list-gate), writing or polishing the emails themselves (wordsmith), inbound SEO (seo-audit, programmatic-seo), WhatsApp/RCS messaging (integrate-whatsapp, sent-two-way-messaging), or finding the market/ICP.
