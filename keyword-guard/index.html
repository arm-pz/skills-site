hreflang hygiene · one page, two languages

A single page mixing English and Arabic with no lang or hreflang declared.

what Google does with that

It treats it as one ambiguous document and dilutes topical authority for both languages. The fix: split into per-language URLs, declare the full variant set on every one of them — reciprocity is mandatory, or Google ignores the whole set — and keep <html lang> as its own signal.

house rules

How it advises, always.

One query, one owner

The page with the most clicks and impressions owns the query. Every rival gets de-optimized and links to the owner instead of competing with it.

Concrete or silent

Recommendations cite data and specific pages — never "improve your content". Every choice is framed through search intent and query ownership.

White-hat, honest about time

No link schemes, no cloaking, no keyword stuffing. Ranked by impact × effort, with a straight warning that SEO compounds over months, not days.
