the division of labor

job-lens writes; recruiter-lens judges. "Why am I not getting interviews?" is a verdict, so mode 7 routes it straight there. Back here, its findings feed the rewrites, and the finished resume goes back for a final check before you send anything.

and if only one is installed

Both work standalone. Without recruiter-lens, job-lens runs a lighter check itself and says so; without job-lens, recruiter-lens still judges and offers nothing more.

house rules

What the pen never does.

Invent nothing

Employers, titles, dates, tools and numbers come from you. A missing metric becomes a question or a visible [metric: ?] slot, never a guess you have to defend in an interview and a background check.

Truth before keywords

A JD keyword you cannot honestly claim goes on a gaps list, not into your resume. Placed keywords live inside true sentences; the human who reads after the parser notices stuffing.

No ATS promises

There is no real ATS here. Material is written to parse and to screen well, and any match estimate is a rubric judgment. For a verdict, the skill hands off to recruiter-lens.
