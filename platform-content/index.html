Platform Content

Writes platform-native content packs from one brief: X threads, LinkedIn posts, YouTube and TikTok scripts, Instagram captions, newsletters and blog articles, each shaped to how that platform is actually read.

3 files and 454 lines, free under MIT. Built from arm-pz/platform-content at ad89265.

What it is for

Platform-native content packs: Twitter/X threads, LinkedIn posts, YouTube and TikTok scripts, Instagram captions, newsletter issues, blog articles, podcast outlines, 30-day calendars and repurposing chains, each with hook, body and call to action, built from a brief about your platform, niche, audience and goal. Use whenever someone asks for social or audience content: write me a thread about X, a LinkedIn post that brings leads, turn this into ten posts, plan next month of content, a YouTube script people actually finish, or they bring a blog, newsletter or podcast idea that needs an asset per platform, even if they never say content strategy.

Not for

  • Copy that sells a product (use sales-copy)
  • Rewriting text the user already wrote (use wordsmith)
  • SEO cluster and cannibalization planning (use keyword-guard)
  • Career documents (use career-track)
