Check Skill Visibility

Checks why individual skill pages are missing from Google and produces URL-level findings for repair.

2 files and 41 lines, free under MIT. Built from arm-pz/check-skill-visibility at 793413b.

What it is for

Diagnose why individual agent skill pages are missing from Google Search even when their catalog or homepage appears. Compare exact-name and task queries with the expected landing pages, verify index status when Search Console is available, and produce page-level findings a companion skill can fix.

Reach for it when

  • Someone sees only the domain, finds a competitor or GitHub page instead of their skill, or can find a skill by name but cannot find it by what it does.

Not for

  • A sitewide SEO sweep (use seo-sweep)
  • Repo-only discoverability (use findable)
  • Applying a completed visibility audit (use fix-skill-visibility)
