Eleven brews on the menu

Skills that hand off to each other.

Each brew is a workflow, an ordered chain: every step receives what the step before it produced, and stops at its own gate. Install caffeinity-routing and describe the outcome; it picks the route and tells you which steps you have not installed yet.

Brew 1 · Pour-over

Build a skill and make it installable

  1. draft-skill or prompts-to-skill
  2. tune-skill
  3. prompts-to-skill-release
  4. skill-to-mod optional

skill-launch runs the first steps as one chain.

Brew 2 · Bloom

Get a repo, skill or plugin found

  1. findable
  2. publish-pipeline
  3. upstream-check

findable-polish and upstream-polish add the rewrite.

Brew 3 · Espresso

Decide whether a site is safe to launch

  1. web-preflight
  2. pre-ship-security-red-team
  3. pre-ship-performance-audit
  4. release-gate-chair or release-gate-ledger or release-gate-validator

ship-sentinel holds git push and deploys until the gate has run.

Brew 4 · Cold brew

Grow organic search traffic

  1. seo-crawl
  2. keyword-guard
  3. wordsmith
  4. pre-ship-performance-audit optional
  5. platform-content optional

seo-polish and keyword-resolver package parts of this route.

Brew 5 · French press

Land a job

  1. recruiter-lens
  2. applicant-kit
  3. application-pilot
  4. career-track optional

It never submits an application for you.

Brew 6 · Cupping

Make a decision

  1. research-brief
  2. data-readout optional
  3. crash-test or do-better
  4. strategy-memo

Brew 7 · Slow drip

Learn a skill

  1. topic-primer
  2. study-system
  3. course-builder optional

course-builder applies when the learner is a class or cohort, not one person.

Brew 8 · Nitro

Run cold outbound

  1. cold-email-list-gate
  2. cold-email-ops
  3. sales-copy

Brew 9 · Moka pot

Launch a one-person product

  1. solo-income-blueprint
  2. site-prompt
  3. production-code
  4. paper-trail
  5. release-gate-chair
  6. platform-content

Step five is the whole of Brew 3 · Espresso; paper-trail gives the gate the policy pages to check.

Brew 10 · Descale

Maintain the skill family

  1. tune-skill
  2. prompts-to-skill-release optional
  3. upstream-check

The nightly health job opens the issue this starts from; a weekly scheduled session runs it and opens PRs.

Brew 11 · Estate roast

Stand up or audit a skill estate

  1. skill-estate

One run over the whole collection in eight gated phases; Brew 10 · Descale keeps it healthy afterwards.
