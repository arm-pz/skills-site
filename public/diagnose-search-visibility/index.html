Diagnose Search Visibility

Diagnoses why a homepage or service page is missing from Google or AI answers, then hands each supported finding to the right repair skill.

2 files and 54 lines, free under MIT. Built from arm-pz/diagnose-search-visibility at cbaa0e1.

What it is for

Diagnose why a website, brand, or service page is missing from Google results or rarely cited in AI search answers. Compare expected URLs with observed results, distinguish crawl and indexing issues from brand ambiguity, query mismatch and citation gaps, and produce evidence-backed findings with repair owners and repeatable checks.

Reach for it when

  • A site appears for its domain but not its brand, a service page is missing for its target query, or AI answers cite competing sources.

Not for

  • Auditing individual agent skill pages (use check-skill-visibility)
  • A complete sitewide SEO repair (use seo-sweep)
  • URL submission alone (use get-indexed)
